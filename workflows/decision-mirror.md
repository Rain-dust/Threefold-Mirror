# Decision Mirror · complete loop

1. **Define** the real question, window, alternatives, constraints, resources and review date. Seek only missing facts that change the decision. Persist Decision when write-back is authorized.
2. **Retrieve Past** with [Past](past.md). Show analogues, differences, counter-evidence and missing history. Don't force every choice into an investment/intensity template.
3. **Update Present** with [Present](present.md). Keep the model small, relevant and time-bounded.
4. **Simulate** with [Future](future.md). Cover actual options with direct evidence; append only after current-model validation.
5. **Reflect across time**: relate history, current constraints and possible consequences. State uncertainty and contradictions without claiming future authority.
6. **Receive the actual choice**. Until then the Decision stays open. An already stated choice needs no repeated approval. Append choice Event linked to Decision, Simulation, option, user statement, action and review date. Never manufacture acceptance.
7. **Record interventions**: did seeing the simulation change scope, schedule or behavior? Append actual testimony about the change. A planned action is not a completed outcome.
8. **Observe later reality**. Append Outcome from actual testimony/reliable evidence, linked to the exact choice/Decision/Simulation and interventions. Do not schedule a future check without the user's request.
9. **Reflect and update** with [Reflection](reflection-correction.md). Preserve alternatives and directly cite Outcome in justified State/Pattern revisions.
10. **Repeat a related question** with a new Decision. Retrieve the new Outcome alongside older evidence and compute new branches from the current model.

Real workflows can remain open awaiting the user's choice or later reality; never fabricate either to claim completion. Only the explicitly fictional Golden Path simulates later outcomes for integration acceptance.
